Prime meridian meets equator here. No land, only ocean, yet an invisible intersection which organizes the entire planet's geography. As there is no stop, Soul Makossa is a recognition of music and it’s ability to travel. This was Africa’s first global pop hit, and is infamous for it’s many many copyright battles. It is the most sampled African song ever, and is a global testament to the influence of African music in American and European rock.

Song: Soul Makossa by Manu Dibango
Cameroonian track that became one of Africa's first global pop hits, later sampled by MJ
