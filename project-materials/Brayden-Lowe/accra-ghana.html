Accra, Ghana: Ghana sits on Prime Meridian and is one of West Africa's most vibrant music cultures, which is central to global afrobeats. Osibisa was formed in London in 1969 by Ghanian / Caribbean musicians, and their sound (African rhythm with rock) made them one of the first African bands to achieve mainstream success in Europe and America. Woyaya exemplifies what the project calls a colonial roundtrip. Ghanaian music traditions travel to London and get recombined with Western rock and exported globally as Afro-Rock, a label that acknowledges and also packages the appeal of their origins.
