01 — The weight each foot carried onto the pitch
April 1982

The Falklands

74 days of war in the South Atlantic. 649 Argentines dead. 255 Britons. A defeat for Argentina the country is still holding when the draw is made for Mexico 1986.

June 22, 1986

The draw produces Argentina – England

Quarterfinal. Azteca Stadium. 114,580 in attendance. The only time the two nations have met in competition since the war.

51st and 55th minutes

A hand. Then a run.

Two goals, four minutes apart, by the same player. One the referee did not see. One FIFA later votes the Goal of the Century. The ball is the only witness who cannot lie about either.

03 — Dispatch
Field notes · Estadio Azteca · June 22, 1986

There is a kind of quiet a press box produces when it doesn't know what it has just seen. The pencils stop. You hear the crowd below still going, and you are not sure what they are celebrating.

The first goal went in at fifty-one minutes. Good angle. I wrote HAND before the referee pointed to the center spot. Then I crossed it out.

Four minutes later he took the ball in his own half and ran — without theater, without hesitation. He passed five Englishmen and I counted them and then it was in the net.

Sixty meters. Eleven seconds. I went to the bathroom after the match and counted eleven seconds out loud in the mirror. It is not a long time. What he did with it was absurd. I've been doing this for twenty years and I don't have a better word.
