03 — Dispatch
Correspondent · Lusail Stadium · December 18, 2022

The stadium is brand new. Every surface is intentional. They have thought of everything except what to do when the match is extraordinary, which it is.

Argentina and France trade goals until the scoreline makes no sense. We go to penalties. A shootout is not sport — it is theater with consequences. Tonight the distinction stops mattering.

When the last kick goes in, the colleagues beside me are from six countries and none of them are writing. We watch a man run toward his teammates. They receive him the way a crowd receives a sound. All at once, and completely.

Thirty-six years ago a man in a blue and white shirt held the same cup. The photographs look identical. I don't know if that's a coincidence or if that's the point. I write both down and let the editors decide.

The ball is on the pitch. It has been there this whole time. It does not know what year it is.
