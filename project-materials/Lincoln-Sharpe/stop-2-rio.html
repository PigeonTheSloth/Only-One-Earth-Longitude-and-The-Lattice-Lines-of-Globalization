02 — Dispatch
Wire dispatch · Maracanã · 16 July 1950

STOP. TWO HUNDRED THOUSAND PEOPLE IN THIS BOWL AND NO SOUND. STOP. GHIGGIA HIT IT AND THE CITY STOPPED. STOP.

I have been a correspondent for fourteen years. I know what large crowds sound like when they decide not to make a sound. This was the body learning something the mind had not yet accepted.

The newspapers were already printed. BRAZIL: WORLD CHAMPIONS. Forty thousand copies. Somewhere in this city a driver is sitting in a cab full of the wrong edition. He will know it in the morning when he opens the door and the headline is wrong.

The ball is still in the net. Nobody has moved it. Nobody in this stadium is looking at it.
