Only One Earth

Longitude & the Lattice Lines of Globalization

Explore how students in Prof Schaberg’s “What’s a Worldview?” class illuminate longitude as a metaphor for a wide range of contradictory, paradoxical, overlapping principles of globalization: colonialism; scientific discovery; global transportation and social media; consumerism; climate change and the industrial revolution.

Can we come together in a common vision of our ONLY ONE EARTH experience? Or will division always fragment our collective view?
